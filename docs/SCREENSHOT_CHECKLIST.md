# Screenshot Checklist

Capture only real application data or clearly marked synthetic test data. Do not publish private customer, account, or credential information.

- Login and logout.
- Admin dashboard.
- Inventory list and filters.
- Spare-part details with part number and stored compatible-model text.
- Add/edit product form.
- Category and supplier pages.
- Barcode scanner fallback and manual entry.
- Stock receipt and stock movement history.
- Sale creation and insufficient-stock result.
- Purchase Order draft, ordered, partial receipt, and completed receipt states.
- Low-stock and out-of-stock indicators.
- Reports and CSV export.
- Demand forecast with 28-day period.
- Market-price observations and stale-age/freshness display.
- Purchase recommendation.
- Selling-price recommendation.
- Inventory Assistant.
- Users and role controls.
- Mobile/small-screen layouts for representative pages.
